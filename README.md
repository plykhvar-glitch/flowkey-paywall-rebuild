# Paywall Audit — flowkey

**Live:** https://plykhvar-glitch.github.io/flowkey-paywall-rebuild/

Five changes to flowkey’s trial paywall (“How Your Free Trial Works” → “Unlock All Access”), built from the ad creatives flowkey already runs and the answers its onboarding already collects. Same sheet, same art direction, same plans at the same prices. Prepared by Adapty, October 2026.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 1 | The paywall opens on the ad they tapped: six ad promises (“One wrong note…”, “Wanna see something cool?”, “Check out this app!”, “Learning piano is not easy”, “Day 1 → Day 7”, “Someday I’ll learn to read music”), six versions of the sheet | CR +15–20% |
| 2 | Plans and the Today / Day 5 / Day 7 timeline on one sheet, with Today naming their first course | CR +10–15% |
| 3 | 4.7 stars from 66K ratings, 15 Million downloads and Yamaha on the sheet, with a quote matched to the ad | CR +10–15% |
| 4 | “Others too” in the quiz opens on the Family Plan | ARPU +5–10% |
| 5 | flowkey Classic ($99.99 / year) as a comparison sheet when they close the paywall | ARPU +10–15% |

The Paywall view shows the three strongest ads (“One wrong note…”, “Check out this app!”, “Learning piano is not easy”) side by side, each phone with its own live paywall. One row of steps moves all three together: 1 · The ad as it ran on Meta, 2 · Today, 3 · Paywall, 4 · Family, 5 · Offer on close. EN / DE / FR, Dark / Light and a 30-second walkthrough sit above the phones.

**Grounding.** US prices from flowkey’s help centre: Premium $24.99 / month or $149.99 / year with a 7-day trial, Family $37.49 / $224.99, Classic $99.99 / year; per-month figures are those prices divided by twelve. 4.7 stars and 66K ratings from the US App Store; “15 Million downloads” and the Yamaha partnership from flowkey’s App Store screenshots; the 14-day money-back guarantee from the help centre. Quotes from flowkey.com and US App Store reviews, as published. Creative groups and impressions from public ad intelligence, 27 July – 24 October 2026, not from flowkey’s ad account. German and French screens are our translation.

*Impact ranges are Adapty’s expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for flowkey.*
